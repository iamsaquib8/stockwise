- Market scans cover whole indices: India defaults to NIFTY 500 (members
  from NSE, else niftyindices.com, else a built-in list) and the US to the
  S&P 500. `picks`, `matrix`, `surprise` and `longterm` take `--universe`.
  Index scans reuse quotes kept in `cache.db` for 15 minutes, and
  `stockwise cache clear` takes a target (`http`, `quotes`, `snapshots`,
  `bars`, `results`, `members`, `bhavcopy` or `all`). Outside Indian market
  hours, `movers` and `heatmap` price every stock from BSE's end-of-day
  bhavcopy ([#431](https://github.com/iamsaquib8/stockwise/pull/431)).
