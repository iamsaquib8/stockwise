- `tax in`, `tax export in`, `tax advance` and `harvest` now decide in one place which sales count as listed shares or equity funds (Sec. 111A/112A). Debt funds, SGBs, crypto and gold, silver, liquid and international ETFs are left out of all four, so a tax-free SGB redemption no longer raises advance tax and a crypto loss no longer reduces STCG tax ([#88](https://github.com/iamsaquib8/stockwise/issues/88)).
