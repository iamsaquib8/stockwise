- `risk` and the volatility in `timeframes` annualize with the right number of bars for the asset: 365 a year for crypto, 260 for FX, 252 for stocks, and 288 five-minute bars a day for crypto. `returns` annualizes by calendar time and shows `—` (null in JSON) for spans under a year, so a +100% year reads +100% for crypto and for stocks. Calmar annualizes over the number of returns ([#73](https://github.com/iamsaquib8/stockwise/issues/73)).
