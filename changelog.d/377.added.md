- Zero-setup persistent cache: without Redis, provider responses are kept in
  a SQLite (WAL) `cache.db` in the data directory, so the next command reuses
  fresh quotes and NAVs instead of fetching them again. New
  `stockwise cache status|clear`, a `cache.db` line in `stockwise doctor`, and
  a `layer="sqlite"` series in `/metrics`. `STOCKWISE_CACHE_DB=off` disables
  it. First slice of
  [#377](https://github.com/iamsaquib8/stockwise/issues/377); market
  snapshots, the task store and the background worker follow.
